# 📊 Tableau Public for Beginners: Data Visualization Basics

This project is part of a guided course for beginners who want to learn how to build business dashboards using **Tableau Public**, a free and powerful data visualization tool. The goal is to take raw data and turn it into clear, interactive, and insightful dashboards for stakeholders.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

In this hands-on project, we cover the **fundamentals of Tableau Public**—from connecting to data, creating visualizations, and building a clean, interactive dashboard layout. The project includes **15 visualizations** built from scratch, including:

* 5 KPI BANs
* 5 Line Charts
* 3 Bar Charts
* 1 Pie Chart
* 1 Map Chart

Each chart is formatted, styled, and customized to match stakeholder-friendly standards.

---

## 🧠 Key Learning Objectives

* Explore and navigate Tableau Public (web & desktop).
* Understand the difference between **Dimensions vs Measures** and **Continuous vs Discrete** fields.
* Create essential chart types: **KPIs**, **Line Charts**, **Bar Charts**, **Pie Charts**, and **Maps**.
* Apply best practices in **formatting, tooltip cleanup, chart layout, and dashboard composition**.
* Publish the final dashboard to **Tableau Public** for sharing and collaboration.

---

## 📌 What I Built

### ✅ KPI BANs (x5)

* Total Sales
* Total Orders
* Total Profit
* Total Customers
* Total Products Sold
  Each BAN was styled with text formatting, alignment, number formatting (in thousands or millions), and tooltips turned off for clarity.

---

### 📈 Line Charts (x5)

Trend lines created monthly for each KPI, with:

* Customized axes and gridlines
* Styled tooltips
* Black color scheme
* Number formatting in thousands
* Zero line emphasized

---

### 📊 Bar Charts (x3)

Built for:

* Sales by Sub-category (with Top Sales highlighted via a calculated field)
* Sales by Category
* Sales by Region

All bar charts include:

* Custom formatting
* Sorted bars
* Data labels
* Highlighted top performer

---

### 🥇 Pie Chart (x1)

* **Sales by Segment**
* Uses color gradients based on sales
* Clean tooltip design
* Number formatting in thousands
* Includes best practices (max 4 slices)

---

### 🗘️ Map Chart (x1)

* **Sales by State**
* Auto-generated geographic map
* Color-coded by sales volume
* Styled tooltips
* Custom currency formatting
